## Ask-Claude prompts

1. "Who invented electricity. How did they do it. How much wealth did they create for themselves and others, why did electricity move into government owned utilities and no one person collected royalties for it. Also tell me, in the US and in Australia, which companies supply electricity and what is their revenue, net profit of the top 3 providers in each country."
   → Nobody invented electricity — it's natural; Faraday's dynamo (1831) made it usable, Edison opened the first power station (Pearl St, 1882) and Tesla/Westinghouse's AC system won; utilities became regulated monopolies because one grid per area is most efficient. Big players: US — NextEra, Duke, Southern Co.; Australia — AGL, Origin, EnergyAustralia.
2. "Who are the largest electricity suppliers in the US, Australia, other parts of the world. How has their revenue and profits grown over 10-15 years. Has it been a good investment – look at the share price. Look at market cap, PE, net debt"
   → NextEra Energy is the largest US utility by market cap (~US$150bn+); in Australia AGL and Origin lead retail; globally State Grid of China is the biggest by revenue.
3. "Who invented the telephone. How did they do it. How much wealth did they create for themselves and others. Why is it that there is no one global telecom giant like there is Google in search or Facebook in social media. Why do most countries have 3-5 telecom players. How did it evolve like that"
   → Alexander Graham Bell patented it in 1876 (Bell Telephone → AT&T); telecom needs national spectrum licences and physical networks, so governments limit each country to a few licensed operators.
4. "Who invented steam engines. How did they do it. What was the unique insight there. How much wealth did they create for themselves and others."
   → Thomas Newcomen built the first practical one (1712); James Watt's separate condenser (patented 1769) made it ~4x more efficient, and Boulton & Watt grew rich on it.
5. "How did steam engines evolve to gas engines, and what did Henry Ford see that others did not and how was he able to commercialise them. Did he read about gas engines in a magazine saying steam engines take over 30 minutes to get started but gas engines are the future. Why was Henry Ford able to succeed and did the inventor of gas engines create wealth for themselves"
   → Nikolaus Otto built the four-stroke engine (1876) and Karl Benz the first car (1886); Ford (who said he read about gas engines in *The World of Science*) won by mass-producing the Model T on a moving assembly line from 1913, cutting its price from $850 to ~$260.

7. "Where does petrol come from. What is fossil fuel, is it oil extracted from fossils of whales and other animals? What is the evolution of oil into petrol or diesel, how did fracking get discovered. Who makes most of the oil in the world and who has created wealth for themselves here"

8. "Who are the largest listed energy companies in the US and in the world. How has their revenue and profits grown over 10-15 years…"
